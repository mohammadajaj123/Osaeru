How coaches use it

A coach's week before a tournament

  1. Entry list is out

    30+ potential opponents

    Every name in the division has a different game, and you can't watch hours of video on each.

  2. Same day

    Upload their matches

    Video from any phone or past competition footage. No special cameras.

  3. Reports arrive

    One report per opponent

    Dominant positions, habits and a counter-plan, ready to share with your athlete.

  4. Training days

    Drill the plan

    Build sessions around the exact positions your athlete will face.

  5. Match day

    Compete with a plan

    Your athlete walks in knowing what the opponent does and how to shut it down.
